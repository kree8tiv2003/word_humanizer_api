HOW TO OPEN SCRIPT STUDIO THE FIRST TIME

1. Drag "Script Studio" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder.
3. Hold the Control key, click "Script Studio", and choose "Open".
4. Click "Open" in the box that appears.

Your Mac shows a warning the first time because the app is not from the App Store.
You only need to do this once. After that, open it normally.

If the box only offers "Move to Trash" or "Done":
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
   and click "Open Anyway" next to Script Studio.
